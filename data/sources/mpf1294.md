<!-- excerpt:mpf-enrol-maintain tags:enrolment,policy -->
Enrolment and Timetabling Policy (MPF1294) v19, effective 22 December 2022. Students must maintain enrolment, including enrolling, re-enrolling and registering for classes in the manner and time required. Students may amend enrolment in certain circumstances.
<!-- excerpt:mpf-withdrawal tags:enrolment,policy,dates -->
Students may amend enrolment in designated periods. Withdrawal after census date and last date to withdraw can result in academic and/or financial penalty unless special circumstances apply. Subjects withdrawn before census will not appear on the transcript nor incur a fee. Special-circumstances withdrawal is out of scope for this agent.
<!-- excerpt:mpf-class-reg tags:timetable,policy -->
Class registration opens at least four weeks before the teaching period. Students must be registered for all classes in subjects in which they are enrolled. The timetable might not allow every preferred combination; students should choose alternative subjects if they cannot resolve a clash. Accepting a clash is not grounds for special consideration. Students may attend only classes for which they have registered.
