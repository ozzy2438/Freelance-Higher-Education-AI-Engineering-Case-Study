Coursework students enrol in subjects by adding them to their study plan in my.unimelb, then Plan and enrol → Schedule → Enrol → Confirm. Enrolled subjects show a green tick. If you cannot enrol, check Handbook eligibility and the Enrolment Assistance website.
<!-- excerpt:enrol-key-dates tags:enrolment,dates -->
Four critical dates (vary by subject in the Handbook): teaching period; last self-enrol date (final date to enrol or change via study plan); subject census date (last date to withdraw without fee liability or transcript entry; enrolment after census is not possible even with coordinator approval); last date to withdraw without fail.
<!-- excerpt:enrol-late-generic tags:enrolment,late_enrol,eaf,late_enrol_generic -->
After the last self-enrol date, late enrolment is not recommended. Email the subject coordinator for written approval, then submit an Enrolment Assistance Form (EAF) with the approved email (PDF preferred) within 72 hours. The attachment must show date and time, student and coordinator emails, subject codes/titles, and clear approval. Enrolling after census is not possible, even with coordinator approval.
