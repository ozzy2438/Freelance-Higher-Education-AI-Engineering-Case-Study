<!-- excerpt:eaf-when tags:enrolment,eaf -->
Use the Enrolment Assistance Form when you cannot enrol or change your study plan in my.unimelb. Examples: empty study plan; add a Handbook subject that does not appear on the plan; enrol after the last self-enrol date with subject-coordinator approval submitted within 72 hours. Average processing time is one working day (operations notice, not a guaranteed SLA).
<!-- excerpt:eaf-late-fbe tags:enrolment,late_enrol,eaf,late_enrol_fbe -->
The Faculty of Business and Economics does not approve enrolment in subjects after Friday of Week 3, even with subject coordinator approval. If you were prevented from enrolling on time for reasons outside your control your application will be assessed but enrolment is not guaranteed. Melbourne Law School does not permit enrolment in BLAW10001 Principles of Business Law after the last self-enrolment date.
<!-- excerpt:eaf-docs tags:enrolment,eaf -->
Late enrolment supporting documentation must be subject-coordinator approval (course-coordinator approval is not accepted for this request type) showing date and time, student name and email, coordinator name and email, subject title and code, and the email conversation.
<!-- excerpt:eaf-who-not tags:enrolment,eaf -->
Melbourne Law School Masters students, graduate researchers requesting candidature change, and some online graduate students should not use the Enrolment Assistance Form; they have different published channels.
